THE IDEA BEHIND THE BOOK

A scientific lens.
A human experience.

In 2023, I moved from India to Germany for a master’s in astrophysics and found myself navigating visa delays, bureaucracy, a breakup, and a demanding new academic system. Missing a semester and scrambling to catch up left me anxious and exhausted. Those experiences led me to read deeply about mental health, habits, and performance, and to start building mental frameworks for someone who thinks like a scientist.

Being a Nerd might save your life is my attempt to turn that experience into a self-development toolkit for academics. Drawing on physics, neuroscience, and related fields, it explores scientific ideas as practical lenses for perfectionism, impostor syndrome, burnout, and everyday academic stress. Each chapter explains a concept, then connects it to real-life decisions and problems, with neuroplasticity—the brain’s ability to change through repeated experiences—as a recurring theme.
